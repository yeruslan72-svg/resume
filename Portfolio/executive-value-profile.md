### SELECTED OPERATIONAL ACHIEVEMENT

**From Decommissioning to 12 More Years of Service**

As a Senior Mechanic, I led the recovery of a 1972 port tug that had become immobilised and was being considered for decommissioning.

Several critical systems were out of service, including main and auxiliary engines, the air compressor, fire pump and ballast pump.

Working with the crew, I organised the recovery effort, mobilised the team and helped secure scarce spare parts, including components recovered from decommissioned vessels.

Following restoration, sea trials demonstrated that the tug was operational. The vessel was retained on the port's balance sheet and returned to service.

It continued performing ship manoeuvring and fuel and water supply operations for another **12 years**.

This experience demonstrates practical asset recovery, team mobilisation, technical coordination and the ability to restore productive value under severe resource constraints.

### AVCS — INDEPENDENT RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

**Adaptive Vector Control System**

I am developing AVCS, a proprietary framework for examining the structural integrity of decision-making in high-risk operational environments.

Its focus includes:

- Decision ownership and authority boundaries.
- Clarity of escalation and intervention pathways.
- Visibility of overrides and operational deviations.
- The relationship between procedures and actual operating conditions.
- The conversion of warnings and information into timely, accountable action.

AVCS is currently at the framework, research and simulation stage. It is intended to complement existing operational, engineering and safety systems — not replace professional judgment, regulatory authority or established safety responsibilities.
