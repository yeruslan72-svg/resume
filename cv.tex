%----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{AVCS -- Independent Research \& Professional Practice}

\textbf{\color{avcsdark}Adaptive Vector Control System} \hfill \textbf{\color{avcsgray}2016 -- Present}

\vspace{2pt}
Developed from accumulated experience in marine, offshore, FSO and control-room operations, AVCS formalizes recurring structural problems observed in high-risk operational environments.

\textbf{AVCS addresses:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=2pt]
    \item Intervention -- whether it is structurally protected and actionable
    \item Decision ownership -- whether it is singular and real-time
    \item Escalation -- whether it is mandatory or interpretive
    \item Critical controls -- whether they remain effective under actual conditions
    \item Procedure vs. reality -- where the two diverge and why
    \item Operational drift -- normalization of deviations over time
    \item Evidence vs. status -- whether claimed conditions can be independently verified
    \item Authority boundaries -- where recommendation ends and authorization begins
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\textit{AVCS does not replace engineering judgment, operational expertise, HSE systems, risk management or regulatory authority. Its purpose is to examine whether existing information, controls and procedures can be converted into timely, authorized and reviewable action when operating conditions change.}
