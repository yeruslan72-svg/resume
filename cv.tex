\section*{Professional Profile}

Operational professional and independent Decision Architect with \textbf{30+ years of progressive experience} across marine, offshore and production operations, including machinery operations, FSO/FPSO production and control-room environments.

Career progression from \textbf{Motorman} and \textbf{Ship Mechanical Technician} to offshore \textbf{CCR/Production Operator} and, subsequently, \textbf{Decision Architect}.

Experienced in operating within environments where decisions must be made under changing conditions, incomplete information, production pressure and time constraints.

Since 2016, independently developing \textbf{AVCS (Adaptive Vector Control System)} — a proprietary framework for examining the structural integrity of operational decision systems.

My work focuses on the gap between controls and procedures on paper and their effectiveness in actual operational conditions, including decision ownership, intervention authority, critical controls, escalation, overrides, operational drift and the ability to maintain control when conditions change.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Core principle:} \textit{Control is the ability to stop in time. If a system cannot be stopped, it is not under control.}

\textbf{\color{avcsdark}Adaptive Vector Control System} \hfill \textbf{\color{avcsgray}2016 – Present}

Independent development of a proprietary decision-integrity framework for high-risk operational environments.

AVCS examines whether an operational decision environment contains the structural conditions necessary to:

\begin{center}
\textbf{Detect $\rightarrow$ Trigger $\rightarrow$ Establish Authority $\rightarrow$ Intervene $\rightarrow$ Act $\rightarrow$ Record}
\end{center}

\textbf{Areas of focus:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=2pt]
    \item Trigger clarity
    \item Decision ownership
    \item Protected intervention
    \item Override transparency
    \item Drift detection
    \item Authority boundaries
    \item Evidence-based decision status
    \item Pre-incident structural vulnerability
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\textit{AVCS does not replace engineering judgement, operational expertise, HSE systems, risk management or regulatory authority. Its purpose is to examine whether existing information, controls and procedures can be converted into timely, authorized and reviewable action when operating conditions change.}

%----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
% OPERATIONAL PROBLEM OF FOCUS
%----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{Operational Problem of Focus}

High-risk organizations rarely lack information. The structural problem is often what happens between information and action.

\textbf{My work focuses on questions such as:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=2pt]
    \item Who owns the decision?
    \item What condition triggers intervention?
    \item Is the authority to stop explicit?
    \item Can intervention occur without retaliation or ambiguity?
    \item What happens when procedure and reality diverge?
    \item What happens when the operational boundary changes faster than the organization can respond?
    \item Can the decision be reconstructed later from evidence?
    \item Can the organization maintain control when conditions become abnormal or time-critical?
\end{itemize}
