02 / Selected Achievement

Asset Recovery Under Severe Constraints

From Decommissioning to 12 More Years of Service

As a Senior Mechanic, I led the recovery effort for a 1972 port tug that had become immobilised and was being considered for decommissioning.

Several critical systems were out of service, including the main and auxiliary engines, air compressor, fire pump and ballast pump.

Working with the crew, I organised the recovery effort, mobilised personnel and helped secure scarce spare parts. Some components were sourced from decommissioned vessels.

Following restoration, successful sea trials demonstrated that the tug was operational. The vessel was retained on the port's balance sheet and returned to service.

12 YEARS Additional service after restoration

The tug continued performing ship manoeuvring and fuel and water supply operations for another 12 years.

This experience demonstrates practical asset recovery, team mobilisation, technical coordination and the ability to restore operational capability under severe resource constraints.

04 / Independent Research & Development

AVCS — Adaptive Vector Control System

AVCS DNA MATRIX

Alongside my operational career, I am developing AVCS, a proprietary framework for examining the structural integrity of decision-making in high-risk operational environments.

Its research focuses on:

  • Decision ownership and authority boundaries.
  • Clarity of escalation and intervention pathways.
  • Visibility of overrides and operational deviations.
  • The relationship between procedures and actual operating conditions.
  • The conversion of warnings and information into timely, accountable action.
Development status: AVCS is at the framework, research and simulation stage. It is intended to complement existing operational, engineering and safety systems — not replace professional judgment, regulatory authority or established safety responsibilities.
